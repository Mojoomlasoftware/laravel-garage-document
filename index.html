Installing on Laragon Local Development

CronJobs

Enable automated scheduler tasks

A CronJob runs the Laravel scheduler every minute — enabling automated reminders, scheduled reports, and time-based notifications.

Important

Without CronJobs, automated reminder emails and scheduled tasks will not run. Set this up immediately after installation.

Add in cPanel → CronJobs

cPanel CronJob Command
* * * * * php /path/to/garage/artisan schedule:run >> /dev/null 2>&1

Setting Up in cPanel

  1. Log in to cPanel → scroll to Advanced → click Cron Jobs
  2. Under "Common Settings", select Once Per Minute (* * * * *)
  3. Paste the command above into the "Command" field, replacing the path
  4. Click Add New Cron Job — the scheduler is now active

F) Stripe Settings

Click on Settings within Stripe Settings to configure your payment gateway. The Garage Master system supports two methods for Stripe integration depending on your country.

Stripe Connection Status

To accept online payments, you must connect your Stripe account to the system. The connection method varies based on your selected country.


Step 1: Select Your Stripe Account Country

First, select your Stripe account country from the dropdown menu. The system will automatically determine the best integration method for your country.


Step 2: Choose Your Integration Method

Based on your selected country, one of the following options will appear:

Option A: Stripe Connect (Recommended for Supported Countries)

If your country supports Stripe Connect (e.g., United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and other supported regions) , you'll see the "Connect Your Stripe Account" button.



Click the button to securely connect your Stripe account. If you don't have a Stripe account yet, click on "Please create stripe account" to set up a new account first.


Once connected successfully, you'll see a confirmation message indicating your Stripe account is active and ready to receive payments:



Note: You can disconnect your Stripe account at any time by clicking the "Disconnect" button.



Option B: Manual API Key Integration (For Non-Stripe Connect Countries)

If your country doesn't support Stripe Connect (e.g., India and other regions) , you'll see a form to manually enter your Stripe API keys:



To enable online payments with manual integration, enter your Stripe API keys below:


  • Stripe Publishable Key

    • This is your public key used for client-side operations.

    (Example: pk_test_gMHgW5FAxW4GUHu1I4gfZzxj or pk_live_...)


  • Stripe Secret Key

    • This is your private key used for server-side operations. Keep this confidential.

    (Example: sk_test_*********** or sk_live_***********)


How to Get Your Stripe API Keys:
  1. Log in to your Stripe Dashboard at https://dashboard.stripe.com
  2. Navigate to Developers → API keys
  3. Copy your Publishable key and Secret key
  4. Paste them into the respective fields above
  5. Click "UPDATE" to save your keys

Important: Use test keys (starting with pk_test_ and sk_test_) during testing, and switch to live keys (starting with pk_live_ and sk_live_) when running in production.



After successfully connecting via either method, your system will be ready to securely process online payments through Stripe.
